Challenge 1 [60 marks]

You are required to develop a 10 level tower game. You need a class named TowerGame to implement the
program. The descriptions of class are as follows:

Implement a 10 level tower game. The game is played between 2 players. The game goes as follows:

 Each player takes turn to draw two random numbers.
 If the addition of these two numbers is an even number, the player may climb one level up the tower. If
the addition of these two numbers is an odd number, the player fall one level down the tower.
 Any player who gets to the top of the tower first will be the winner.

You are required to implement the necessary methods in your implementation.
